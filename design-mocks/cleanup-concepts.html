Priority Keep the inset bar, drop the outline

A priority border wraps a second color around a goal that already has a type color, and on Day it is pinned to the card edge so the rounded corner gets a square cap. Take the border off calendar events and network nodes (make the event border the same as its fill). Keep priority as the inset bar from the reference, and only paint High. Medium and Low are the common case; a bar on every row is what makes the column look noisy.
